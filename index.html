About Main In Motion

Main In Motion is an $8M streetscape improvement project covering 8 blocks of Main Street (SR 42) in downtown Mooresville, Indiana — from Monroe Street to Maple Lane at the railroad tracks. Funded by tax increment financing through the Mooresville Redevelopment Commission.
